How to Play

  • Swap adjacent orbs to match 3 or more.
  • Every 2 valid swaps, a cosmic question appears.
  • Correct answer → the counter resets to 2 swaps.
  • Wrong answer → the counter drops by 1, so the next question comes sooner.
  • Hit the target score to unlock the next sector.
  • If you run out of moves before the target, the mission ends.

Scoring

  • Match-3 = 30 points
  • Match-4 = 60 points
  • Match-5+ = 100 points
  • Cascades = +50% per chain level

Warp

  • Each sector gives you 1 warp.
  • Use it if the board gets stuck with no valid moves.
